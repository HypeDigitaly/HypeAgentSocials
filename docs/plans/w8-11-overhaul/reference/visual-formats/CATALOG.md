### Top-5 insights

1. **Faces still dominate reach, but the #1 item has no camera at all.** 14/30 top slots are talking-head
   variants — yet the single highest weighted score (51.1) is an AI-generated photoreal drama scene with a
   caption overlay (@aimaster.labs, 2.17M views from a 6.2K-follower account). Photoreal AI imagery is
   already outperforming at the very top of this niche.
2. **Photoreal-scene-with-caption-overlay is the strongest faceless class we can actually generate.**
   4/30 top slots, median 747K views, and it also wins in the slideshow slice (@aitools_guy: 909K views,
   6.6% save rate, +42.5) with the same recipe: real-looking minimalist photo + short clean text block.
   This directly justifies adding a photoreal/lifestyle-photo style class next to designed cards.
3. **Designed cards are the bottom of the observed range.** The two items closest to our current output —
   @riseinstitute.tech's typography title card (1.9K views) and @launch.automation's polished product-render
   promo (1.9K views, negative weighted score, though 41% like rate suggests boosted/niche delivery) — are
   the weakest performers among all targets studied. Designed cards read as ads; photoreal reads as content.
4. **Screen-recording/tutorial is the reliable B2B workhorse** (10/30 slots, median ~599K): "watch AI do
   the thing" (build a website, generate a game, run a dashboard). @launch.automation's only hit (+29.5,
   167K views vs their usual 2-5K) was exactly this — a dashboard screen-recording with an AI-rendered
   sci-fi cover — evidence that *pairing an AI-render hook image with product-demo content* is the winning
   combination for small accounts.
5. **Hooks are text-first everywhere.** Nearly every top-30 item, regardless of class, opens with a bold
   text overlay hook (tutorial_promise, bold_claim, question, shock_statement). For an image engine this
   means: the caption/hook layer matters as much as the background class, and every new style class needs
   a strong text-overlay slot to compete.
